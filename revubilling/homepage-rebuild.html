The short version

Four things are costing you calls

01 The buttons ask for the wrong thing

"Request A Quote" and "Schedule A Demo" both ask for commitment before the visitor has a reason to give it — and there's nothing to demo. RevuBilling is a service staffed by people.

02 It names the wrong pain

"Bottom line" and "revenue cycle" describe a category, not a bad Tuesday. The real pain is forty minutes on hold and an appeal resubmitted three times.

03 It's written in billing-industry language

ERA processing, days in A/R, HCPCS, clean-claim scrubbing. Don't delete the terms — that's your proof of competence. Demote them to caption lines.

04 The credibility is buried

20+ years, 400+ practices, HIPAA, certified coders — all below the fold. That belongs in the visitor's first eight seconds.

Build order

Three passes, not twelve

The section numbers above are page order. This is build order — Pass 1 ships on its own without waiting for photos or testimonials.

PASS 1

Text swaps — no new components

1–2 days
  1. Sections 01, 02, 05, 06, 09, 12 — replace copy in the existing CMS fields.
  2. Remove "Contact Us" from the nav, add the phone number in its place.
  3. Change the nav button sitewide to "Talk to a Specialist" — check it isn't hardcoded on landing pages.
  4. Link the five specialty chips to their pages.
  5. Add the sticky tap-to-call bar on mobile.
Owner: Web / CMS  ·  Done when: the words "demo" and "quote" appear nowhere on the homepage, and one CTA wording repeats in the nav, hero, and footer.
PASS 2

New sections

1 week
  1. Build Section 03 (proof strip), 04 (Sound Familiar), 07 (How It Works), 11 (FAQ).
  2. Build Section 10 with placeholder photos — don't publish it empty.
  3. Re-order the page to the section numbering above.
  4. Add FAQPage structured data.
Owner: Web + Design  ·  Done when: all twelve sections are live in order and legible at 375px with no horizontal scroll.
PASS 3

Content that has to be gathered

2–3 weeks, runs in parallel — start now
  1. Document every VERIFY number: 97% first-pass, 400+ practices, 20+ years, 2–3 week onboarding, 30+ EHRs. Source and date for each. Cut what can't be shown.
  2. Re-collect three testimonials with name, role, specialty, city — plus written release for each.
  3. Write one mini case study with before/after numbers.
  4. Headshots and credentials for two or three team members.
  5. Confirm the pricing-model line and the staffing answer for the FAQ.
  6. Compliance sign-off on all HIPAA / BAA / security wording.
Owner: Marketing, Ops, Compliance  ·  Done when: every placeholder from Pass 2 is filled with a documented, permissioned asset.
Do this on day one, not at the end

Record a 30-day analytics baseline before Pass 1 goes live, and tag every CTA by position (nav / hero / footer). Use a call-tracking number so phone conversions get attributed. Without a baseline there's no way to prove any of this worked — and the hero button change is the one most likely to move, so it's worth measuring properly.

Before you publish

Nothing ships until all of this is true

  • The words "demo" and "request a quote" appear nowhere on the homepage — buttons, forms, and thank-you page included.
  • Every statistic has a documented source and a stated measurement period.
  • One CTA wording is used in the nav, hero, and footer — identical text in all three.
  • A phone number is visible in the header and reachable in one tap on mobile.
  • Every testimonial carries a name, role, specialty, and city, with permission on file.
  • No placeholder photo, logo, or quote is still on the page.
  • Every industry term appears only in a caption line — never in a heading or a first sentence.
  • Compliance has approved every HIPAA, BAA, and data-security statement.
  • Analytics baseline recorded and CTA events firing correctly in a live test.
  • Page title and meta description updated to match the new positioning.
  • All five specialty chips link to live pages.
One competitor, for reference

What eAssist does that you don't

Their live hero: "Most dental practices leave money on the table. With eAssist's billing pros and AI-Powered Copilot, you'll collect more, reduce A/R headaches, and see lasting growth in as little as 60 days." Their CTA is "Book a consultation" — not a demo, not a quote.

Take: they open on the reader's loss, they attach a timeframe to the outcome, and their button asks for a conversation.

Don't take: they lead with an AI product. Your differentiator is the opposite — a named human who picks up the phone. "Talk to a specialist, not a bot" is a direct counter-position to the category leader. Lean into it.
