## About the product

**What are Prismo Crowns?**
Prismo Crowns are patented colored stainless steel crowns designed for
pediatric dentistry. They are made from the same type of stainless steel used
for traditional pediatric crowns, with the color created through our patented
laser-assisted process. They are not painted, dyed, or plated.

**Are they prefabricated / preformed?**
Yes. Prismo Crowns are prefabricated pediatric stainless steel crowns that come
in a full range of primary tooth sizes. They are placed using the same
preparation and cementation as traditional stainless steel crowns.

**What sizes are available?**
The crowns are available for primary teeth in sizes 2 through 7.

**Is the sizing similar to 3M stainless steel crowns?**
Yes. The sizing is comparable to 3M crowns, and some providers have also found
the sizing comparable to Hu-Friedy. Providers can use their existing size
selection habits with the same clinical workflow.

**What colors are available?**
Our current color options include Unicorn, Gold, Blue, and Purple.
Availability may vary because some colors are produced seasonally or in
limited quantities.

**Are they painted or coated with dye?**
No. Prismo Crowns are not painted or dyed. The color is created through our
patented laser-assisted process and is integrated into the surface of the
stainless steel crown.

**Are the gold crowns gold plated?**
No. The Gold Prismo Crowns are not gold plated. The gold appearance is created
through our patented laser-assisted color process.

## Clinical

**Do they require a different preparation or cement?**
No. Prismo Crowns use the same tooth preparation, crown adjustment, cement,
and placement technique as traditional stainless steel crowns. There is no new
clinical system or additional equipment required.

**Are they billed differently from silver crowns?**
Prismo Crowns use the same clinical procedure and the same dental billing code
as traditional stainless steel crowns. Coverage and reimbursement may vary by
insurance plan and jurisdiction, so providers should confirm the requirements
applicable to their practice.

## Regulatory

**Are they CE marked?**
Yes. Prismo Crowns are CE marked as Class I non-sterile medical devices under
the European Union Medical Device Regulation. For Class I non-sterile devices,
CE marking is completed through the manufacturer's conformity assessment and
EU Declaration of Conformity. A separate notified-body approval or certificate
is not required for this classification.

**Can I get the SDS (Safety Data Sheet), CE Declaration of Conformity, IFU, or
other documentation?**
Yes — these documents are provided by our customer service team. The chat
conversation is forwarded to customer service, and they will email the

## Kits, ordering, and refills

**What kits are available?**
For providers who would like to begin with less commitment, the Intro Kit
includes 48 crowns in selected sizes (the most commonly used sizes 3, 4,
and 5), with two crowns per slot. The Pro Kit includes 240 crowns, with five
crowns in each tooth and size combination.

**Can I customize a kit (only certain colors or sizes)?**
Kits come in a fixed assortment and cannot be customized. For specific
color/size combinations, refill packs are the way to go: each refill pack
contains five crowns in the selected tooth, size, and color, available through
our website. For custom or bulk configurations, the request is forwarded to
customer service.

**Do you sell individual crowns?**
We do not sell individual crowns. Our minimum refill option is a pack of five
crowns in the selected tooth, size, and color. Refill packs are available
through our website.

**Can I receive free samples?**
At this time, we are not providing free samples. Prismo Crowns use the same
stainless steel material and clinical workflow as traditional stainless steel
crowns, so there is no new preparation or placement technique to learn. For
providers who would like to begin with less commitment, the Intro Kit is the
best starting option. *(Chatbot: for institutional evaluation / quality
committee requests, also forward to customer service — see system prompt
rule 5.)*

**I'm a parent — can I buy Prismo Crowns for my child?**
Prismo Crowns are sold to dental practices, not directly to families. Ask your
pediatric dentist about Prismo Crowns — and if they don't carry them yet, you
can share prismocrowns.com with the practice, or we can send them information
directly. *(Chatbot: offer to forward the parent's message to the team.)*

## Shipping

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, we ship internationally to many countries. Shipping options and rates are
calculated based on the destination and selected products at checkout. Our
crowns are packaged in compact, lightweight packs to help simplify
international shipping. Confirmed examples include Mexico and India.

**Do you ship to my specific country?**
Most countries are served; a small number of regions may be affected by trade
or shipping restrictions. If a visitor asks about a country you cannot
positively confirm, do not guess and do not repeat a generic answer — tell
them the customer service team will confirm availability for their country by
email. *(Chatbot: see system prompt rule 4.)*

**Do you have a distributor in my country?**
At this time, we do not have an authorized distributor network. Orders can be
placed directly through our website, and we ship internationally where service
is available.
